## 🏷️ Visa Types and Categories

### Short-Stay Visas (C Visas)

#### Tourist Visa

**Purpose**: For individuals visiting Ireland for leisure or sightseeing
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: Up to 90 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Business Visa

**Purpose**: For business meetings, conferences, contract negotiations
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: Up to 90 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Conference/Event Visa

**Purpose**: For attending specific conferences or events
**Processing Time**: 6-10 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of event + 7 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Exam Visa

**Purpose**: For sitting examinations in Ireland
**Processing Time**: 6-8 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of exam period + 7 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Employment (Atypical Working Scheme) Visa

**Purpose**: For short-term employment under specific schemes
**Processing Time**: 10-14 weeks
**Validity**: Up to 90 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Internship Visa (Short-term)

**Purpose**: For short-term internships up to 3 months
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of internship + 7 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Join Ship Visa

**Purpose**: For seafarers joining vessels in Irish ports
**Processing Time**: 4-6 weeks
**Validity**: 30 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Marriage/Civil Partnership Visa

**Purpose**: For entering marriage or civil partnership in Ireland
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: 90 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Medical Treatment Visa

**Purpose**: For receiving medical treatment in Ireland
**Processing Time**: 6-10 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of treatment + 30 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Performance/Tournament Visa

**Purpose**: For participating in performances or tournaments
**Processing Time**: 6-8 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of event + 7 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Training Visa (Short-term)

**Purpose**: For attending training courses
**Processing Time**: 8-10 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of training + 7 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry)

#### Visit (Family/Friends) Visa

**Purpose**: For visiting family or friends in Ireland
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: Up to 90 days
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

### Long-Stay Visas (D Visas)

#### Critical Skills Employment Permit

**Purpose**: For highly skilled occupations on Critical Skills list
**Processing Time**: 12-16 weeks
**Validity**: 2 years (renewable)
**Fee**: €1,000

#### General Employment Permit

**Purpose**: For shortage occupations
**Processing Time**: 12-16 weeks
**Validity**: 2 years (renewable)
**Fee**: €1,000

#### Intra-Company Transfer Permit

**Purpose**: For multinational company transfers
**Processing Time**: 10-14 weeks
**Validity**: Up to 2 years
**Fee**: €1,000

#### Other Employment Permits

**Purpose**: Contract for Services, Reactivation, Exchange Agreement, Sport and Cultural, Internship
**Processing Time**: 8-16 weeks
**Validity**: Varies by permit type
**Fee**: €500-€1,500

#### Study Visa

**Purpose**: For full-time ILEP courses
**Processing Time**: 8-12 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of course + 3 months
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Join Family Visa

**Purpose**: For family members of Irish citizens/residents
**Processing Time**: 12-16 weeks
**Validity**: 1 year (renewable)
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Lay Volunteer Visa

**Purpose**: For unpaid voluntary work with registered organizations
**Processing Time**: 10-14 weeks
**Validity**: 1 year (renewable)
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Minister of Religion Visa

**Purpose**: For pastoral duties with recognized religious organizations
**Processing Time**: 12-16 weeks
**Validity**: 1 year (renewable)
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### Scientific Researcher Visa

**Purpose**: For research under hosting agreement
**Processing Time**: 10-14 weeks
**Validity**: Duration of research + 3 months
**Fee**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

## 📋 Document Categories and Priority Levels

### Priority Level Definitions

- **🔴 CRITICAL (Must Have)**: Legally mandated documents - automatic rejection if missing
- **🟡 RECOMMENDED (Good to Have)**: Strongly recommended - significantly improves approval chances
- **🔵 OPTIONAL (Can Have)**: Scenario-specific documents - may be required based on circumstances
- **🟢 ENHANCEMENT (Strategic)**: Additional supporting documents - strengthens application

## 🎯 Tourist Visa Document Checklist

### 🔴 CRITICAL - Must Have Documents

#### 1. Application Summary Form (AVATS)

- **Document Type**: Form
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Confirms your online application details
- **Risk of Omission**: Automatic rejection
- **Notes**: Must be printed and signed after online submission

#### 2. Valid Passport

- **Document Type**: Identity Document
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Primary identification and travel document
- **Validity Requirement**: Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond intended stay
- **Risk of Omission**: Automatic rejection
- **Additional Notes**: Must have at least 2 blank pages

#### 3. Two Passport-Sized Photographs

- **Document Type**: Photo
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Recent color photographs for identification
- **Specifications**: 35mm x 45mm, white background, taken within last 6 months
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection

#### 4. Visa Application Fee Receipt

- **Document Type**: Payment Proof
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Confirms payment for visa processing
- **Risk of Omission**: Application may not be processed
- **Amount**: €60 (single entry), €100 (multiple entry)

#### 5. Proof of Financial Means

- **Document Type**: Financial Evidence
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Demonstrates ability to cover expenses during stay
- **Requirements**: Last 6 months of bank statements
- **Minimum Amount**: €3,000 or equivalent
- **Risk of Omission**: High risk of rejection

#### 6. Travel Itinerary

- **Document Type**: Travel Plan
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Shows planned travel dates and destinations
- **Contents**: Flight bookings, accommodation details, planned activities
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection

#### 7. Accommodation Details

- **Document Type**: Lodging Proof
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Verifies lodging arrangements for duration of stay
- **Options**: Hotel booking confirmation OR invitation letter from host
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection

#### 8. Travel Insurance

- **Document Type**: Insurance Policy
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Covers medical emergencies and repatriation
- **Minimum Coverage**: €30,000
- **Risk of Omission**: May be denied entry

#### 9. Letter of Application

- **Document Type**: Cover Letter
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Explains purpose of visit and confirms intention to return
- **Length**: 1-2 pages
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection

#### 10. Proof of Ties to Home Country

- **Document Type**: Supporting Evidence
- **Required**: Yes
- **Description**: Demonstrates compelling reasons to return home
- **Examples**: Employment letter, property documents, family ties
- **Risk of Omission**: High risk of rejection

### 🟡 RECOMMENDED - Good to Have Documents

#### 11. Previous Travel History

- **Document Type**: Travel Records
- **Required**: No (Recommended)
- **Description**: Supports compliance with other visas and travel history
- **Examples**: Previous visa stamps, travel documents
- **Risk of Omission**: May affect credibility assessment

#### 12. Employment Letter

- **Document Type**: Employment Verification
- **Required**: No (Recommended)
- **Description**: Shows current job status and approved leave
- **Contents**: Job title, salary, leave approval, return date
- **Risk of Omission**: Weakens return intent demonstration

#### 13. Invitation Letter from Host in Ireland

- **Document Type**: Support Letter
- **Required**: No (Recommended)
- **Description**: Confirms local support and accommodation arrangements
- **Contents**: Host details, relationship, accommodation offer
- **Risk of Omission**: Affects accommodation proof strength

### 🔵 OPTIONAL - Scenario-Specific Documents

#### 14. Parental Consent Letter (for minors)

- **Document Type**: Legal Consent
- **Required**: Conditional (if applicable)
- **Description**: Required if child travels alone or with one parent
- **Requirements**: Notarized consent from non-traveling parent(s)
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection for minor applicants

#### 15. Marriage Certificate

- **Document Type**: Civil Status
- **Required**: Conditional (if applicable)
- **Description**: Validates relationship when traveling with spouse
- **Requirements**: Official marriage certificate
- **Risk of Omission**: Could delay processing for couples

#### 16. Proof of Enrollment (for students)

- **Document Type**: Academic Status
- **Required**: Conditional (if applicable)
- **Description**: Confirms academic status and ties to home country
- **Requirements**: Letter from educational institution
- **Risk of Omission**: Weakens application for student applicants

#### 17. Medical Reports

- **Document Type**: Medical Documentation
- **Required**: Conditional (if applicable)
- **Description**: Needed if purpose includes medical treatment
- **Requirements**: Medical reports, treatment plans
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection for medical tourism

#### 18. Criminal Record Certificate

- **Document Type**: Background Check
- **Required**: Conditional (if applicable)
- **Description**: Required if criminal history exists
- **Requirements**: Police clearance certificate
- **Risk of Omission**: Delay or rejection if history exists

### 🟢 ENHANCEMENT - Strategic Supporting Documents

#### 19. Cover Letter from Travel Agent or Tour Company

- **Document Type**: Third-party Verification
- **Required**: No (Enhancement)
- **Description**: Helps verify legitimacy of travel plans if booked via agent
- **Risk of Omission**: Low impact unless itinerary credibility is questioned

#### 20. Proof of Civil Status

- **Document Type**: Personal Status
- **Required**: No (Enhancement)
- **Description**: Supports family composition and home ties
- **Risk of Omission**: Moderate impact in family-dependent cases

#### 21. Letter from School/College (for students on break)

- **Document Type**: Academic Verification
- **Required**: No (Enhancement)
- **Description**: Confirms academic enrollment and expected return
- **Risk of Omission**: Moderate impact for young applicants

#### 22. Property or Lease Documents

- **Document Type**: Asset Proof
- **Required**: No (Enhancement)
- **Description**: Shows home country asset ties
- **Risk of Omission**: Low impact but strengthens case

#### 23. Return Flight Booking Confirmation

- **Document Type**: Travel Proof
- **Required**: No (Enhancement)
- **Description**: Proves intent to exit Ireland
- **Risk of Omission**: Not mandatory but helpful for credibility
